# Current status

## Internal alpha

Tridim Business is in active development as an internal alpha. The core workflow includes customer and quotation records, linked jobs, time review, synthetic expense claims, invoicing, manual payment recording, finance exports, and a sandbox-only payment attempt flow.

## Availability

- No public hosted customer service is available.
- The app is not ready for production use.
- Use synthetic data only.
- Live payment settlement, receipt uploads, tax calculation, payroll, and a complete accounting ledger are not available.
- Compliance and market requirements have not been validated for any jurisdiction.

The product is being designed with the intention to support businesses across markets. That is a direction, not a statement of present availability or verified suitability in any country.
